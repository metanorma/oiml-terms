# G 18 — Alphabetical List of Terms Defined in OIML Recommendations and Documents

The **Alphabetical List of Terms Defined in OIML Recommendations and Documents** is published by the
International Organization of Legal Metrology (OIML) as **OIML G 18:2010**.

This guide lists the terms and their definitions as they have been included in the "Terminology" sections
of current OIML Recommendations and Documents in alphabetical order.

It is primarily intended as an aid to OIML Secretariats when developing terminology in drafting OIML
Publications under their responsibility.

## About this online version

This version of the Alphabetical List of Terms (OIML G 18:2010) is an
online, interactively browsable product based on the document published by the
OIML. The publication may be downloaded free of charge from the
[OIML website](https://www.oiml.org).

This online version has the following features:

- all term definitions and notes from OIML G 18:2010 are included without modification,
- terms are listed alphabetically and can be browsed or searched,
- each entry includes the source OIML Recommendation or Document reference,
- terms defined in multiple OIML publications are listed as separate entries.

## Source

The terminology is sourced from **OIML G 18:2010**, which compiles definitions from:

- OIML International Recommendations (OIML R)
- OIML International Documents (OIML D)
- OIML International Guides (OIML G)
- OIML Basic Publications (OIML B)

## About OIML

The [International Organization of Legal Metrology (OIML)](https://www.oiml.org)
is an intergovernmental treaty organization that promotes the global harmonization
of legal metrology procedures. Founded in 1955, OIML develops International
Recommendations and Documents that provide model regulations for measuring
instruments and measurement standards.
